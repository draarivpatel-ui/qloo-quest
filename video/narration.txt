QlooQuest is a cultural detective mystery game powered by Qloo Taste AI cross domain affinity graphs and a four agent investigative crew.
When a priceless cultural artifact disappears across a global circuit of record shops, arthouse cinemas, night markets, and literary salons, players interrogate witnesses within a strict six action investigation budget.
Every witness statement reveals a cultural preference across music, film, dining, literature, fashion, or travel.
Behind the scenes, our Taste Profiler, Cross Domain Linker, Alibi Verifier, and Chief Inspector agents compute cosine similarity across eight dimensional cultural taste vectors and Qloo insights endpoints.
The interactive network canvas visualizes cross domain bridges linking each clue to the six suspects, while the posterior probability matrix updates in real time after every interrogation.
Players can step through the autonomous agent solver, inspect the full reasoning trace, or file a formal warrant naming the culprit and destination city.
